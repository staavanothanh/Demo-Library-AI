# Payments policy

## Accepted payment methods

The production store is intended to accept Visa, Mastercard, American Express, and supported digital wallets. The available methods, currency, taxes, and any authorization hold are displayed before an order is submitted. We do not accept cash by mail, bank passwords, or payment details sent through chat or email.

## Authorization, capture, and receipts

When an order is submitted, the payment provider may authorize the amount before the store captures it. A receipt is sent to the email address on the order. An authorization that expires or is declined does not confirm an order; please do not submit the same order repeatedly while a provider is still processing it.

## Pricing and tax

Book prices are shown in the store currency and can change before checkout. Applicable sales tax and delivery charges are calculated from the delivery address and shown in the order total before confirmation. A promotion cannot be combined with another offer unless its terms explicitly allow it.

## Payment security and disputes

Never share a full card number, security code, one-time passcode, password, or banking credentials with support or the chatbot. If you see an unfamiliar charge, contact the store first with only the order number and the last four digits of the card, then contact your payment provider if needed.

## Demo environment note

This project is a bookstore demo. Its checkout is simulated: it does not collect card details, create a real order, charge money, or contact a payment provider. Do not enter real payment information into the demo.
