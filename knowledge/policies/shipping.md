# Shipping and delivery policy

## Processing and delivery times

Orders are normally prepared within 1–2 business days. Standard delivery is estimated at 3–5 business days after dispatch. Express delivery, when shown at checkout, is estimated at 1–2 business days after dispatch. Delivery estimates are not guarantees and may change during holidays, severe weather, carrier disruption, or other events outside our control.

## Delivery charges

The shipping options and prices shown at checkout are the final shipping charges for the order. Standard delivery is free for orders over $50 before tax; otherwise the standard rate is $4.99. Express delivery is priced separately and is available only for eligible destinations and items.

## Address changes and tracking

Please review the delivery address before submitting an order. We can request an address correction only before the order is handed to the carrier; an address change is not guaranteed. A tracking link is sent when the carrier accepts the parcel. If tracking has not moved for five business days, contact support with the order number.

## Missing or damaged parcels

Report a missing, damaged, or incorrect delivery within seven days of the expected delivery date. Include the order number and clear photographs of damaged packaging or items. We will investigate with the carrier and offer a replacement or another remedy allowed by the returns policy.

## Demo environment note

This project is a bookstore demo. Its checkout does not create a real shipment or carrier label and does not charge a payment method; the timings above describe the intended store policy for a production version.
