# Returns and refunds policy

## Return window

You may request a return within 30 calendar days after delivery. Books must be in resalable condition, with no writing, highlighting, water damage, or missing parts. Please keep the packing slip or order number so that we can locate the purchase.

## Non-returnable items

Personalized or signed-to-order items, gift cards, downloadable products, and items marked final sale cannot be returned. A damaged parcel or an item sent in error is handled as a delivery issue and does not reduce your return rights.

## How to request a return

Contact support before sending anything back. Tell us the order number, the title being returned, the reason, and whether the package is unopened or damaged. We will provide return instructions when the request is approved. Do not mail a return to the billing address without an authorization from support.

## Refund timing and return shipping

For an eligible return, the refund is sent to the original payment method after the item is received and inspected. Most refunds appear within 5–10 business days after approval, depending on the payment provider. We cover return shipping for an incorrect, defective, or damaged item; other returns may be deducted the original shipping charge or the customer may pay return postage.

## Demo environment note

This project is a bookstore demo. It does not create real orders, accept returned parcels, or process refunds; these rules describe the intended production-store workflow.
