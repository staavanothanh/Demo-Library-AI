# Privacy policy

## Information we use

For a production store, we use account details, order and delivery information, payment-provider references, support messages, and essential security logs to provide the service. We collect only the information needed for the selected feature. Card numbers and security codes are handled by the payment provider rather than stored by the bookstore.

## How information is used

We use information to authenticate members, fulfill and support orders, prevent abuse, send service messages, and improve the catalog. We do not use a support conversation to invent book facts or to reveal another customer's information. Public book metadata may be used by the recommendation and search features.

## Chatbot safety

The chatbot is limited to supplied store-policy context and canonical public book data. Do not send passwords, full payment details, authentication codes, session identifiers, cookies, private account information, or confidential business data. If a question requires account access, contact support through the authenticated store flow instead.

## Retention, access, and security

We retain account and order records only for as long as needed for service, legal, accounting, and fraud-prevention purposes. You may request access, correction, or deletion where applicable; some records may need to be retained by law. We use access controls, encrypted transport, and provider safeguards, but no online service can guarantee absolute security.

## Demo environment note

This project is a bookstore demo and is not a production privacy service. Do not enter real personal, payment, or authentication data into the demo, and do not treat demo chatbot responses as a privacy notice for a real purchase.
