# Customer support policy

## Contact and response times

Email support at support@leafandlogic.example or use the authenticated contact form. We aim to acknowledge messages within one business day and resolve ordinary order questions within three business days. Response times can be longer during holidays, carrier disruptions, or large product launches.

## What to include

For an order question, include the order number, the email address used for the order, the affected title, and a short description of the problem. For a damaged or incorrect delivery, add photographs of the parcel and item. Never include a password, full card number, security code, one-time passcode, cookie, session identifier, or other secret.

## Escalation

Support can help with delivery status, returns, cancellations, payment-provider references, account access, and catalog issues. A support specialist may ask for additional non-sensitive details to verify an order. If an issue involves an unauthorized charge or suspected account compromise, contact the payment provider or reset the account password immediately as well as notifying support.

## Chatbot limits

The chatbot can answer from the indexed store policies and public catalog data. It cannot see a private order, issue a refund, change an address, reveal credentials, or guarantee a delivery date. Ask support for account-specific assistance.

## Demo environment note

This project is a bookstore demo. The support address and service-level targets are illustrative, and no real ticket, order, payment, or account action is created by the demo.
